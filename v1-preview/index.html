From customer request to booked job.

The core workflow is intentionally simple so a team can start using it without weeks of setup or training.

STEP 1

Customer reaches out

A customer submits a secure request through the business-specific Gatebrook page.

STEP 2

Lead is captured

The inquiry appears in the correct dashboard and the business receives a notification.

STEP 3

Team follows up

The lead is moved through the pipeline as the business contacts and qualifies the customer.

STEP 4

Owner sees results

Booking conversion, pipeline value, and booked job value update as work progresses.
